\chapter{The internal hom \texorpdfstring{$\lin$}{->}}\label{ch:linhom}

This chapter formalises paper \S5.1 and the parts of \S5.2 used by the
kernel-embedding anchor: the \emph{concrete} construction of the internal hom $C \lin D$
of two integrable cones as a $\Cone$, then as an $\MCone$, then as an
$\ICone$ (paper Lemma~5.4). The chapter also covers the function-level
part of \S5.2: the bijection between bilinear maps $C_1, C_2 \to D$
and curried linear maps $C_1 \to (C_2 \lin D)$. All material in this
chapter lives under \texttt{theories/homs/linhom.v}.

\paragraph{Strategy: concrete vs SAFT.}
Paper \S5 opens with the remark that the cartesian closed structure
on $\ICones$ can be obtained \emph{either} concretely (constructing
$C \lin D$ as a set of integrable linear maps, with its own norm and
its own measurability structure) \emph{or} abstractly via Freyd's
SAFT-style argument applied to the limit-preserving functor
$C \lin -\;$. The paper chooses the concrete route for the internal
hom and reserves the abstract argument for the tensor product
$\otimes$ and the exponential $\mathord{!}$, both delivered (axiom-free)
in Chapters~\ref{ch:tensor} and~\ref{ch:exponential}. The Rocq
formalisation follows the same split: the internal hom $\lin$ is built
concretely in \texttt{theories/homs/linhom.v}; $\otimes$ and
$\mathord{!}$ are then constructed via the concrete M-SAFT machinery of
\texttt{theories/icones/representable.v} on top of $\lin$.

\section{The carrier \texorpdfstring{$C \lin D$}{C -> D}}\label{sec:linhom-carrier}

\begin{definition}[Def 5.2, integrable linear maps]\label{def:linhom-car}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_car}\rocqok\uses{def:icone}
Let $C, D$ be integrable cones (\autoref{ch:icones}). The cone
$C \lin D$ of \emph{integrable linear maps} has as elements the
functions $f : C \to D$ such that, for some $\varepsilon > 0$, the
scaled map $\varepsilon f$ lies in $\ICones(C, D)$. Equivalently,
$f$ is $\Rnn$-linear, $\omega$-continuous, bounded on the unit ball,
measurable in the sense of $\MCones$, and preserves Pettis integrals.
The algebraic structure (zero, addition, scalar multiplication) is
pointwise.
\end{definition}

In Rocq we use a two-layer record. The intermediate record
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_pre} packages the first four fields
(linearity, $\omega$-continuity, ball-boundedness, measurable-path
preservation); the full record
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_car} adds Pettis-integral
preservation as a fifth field. The split is required because the
type of the integral-preservation field mentions the
path-preservation field of \texttt{linhom\_pre}, mirroring the
\texttt{cones\_hom} $\to$ \texttt{mcones\_hom} $\to$ \texttt{icones\_hom}
record layering of M1/M2/M3.

\paragraph{Pointwise algebraic structure.}
The zero, the sum and the non-negative scalar action on the carrier
are pointwise. They live as standalone definitions
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_zero},
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_add},
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_scale}, each packaged as a full
\texttt{linhom\_car} (linearity / continuity / boundedness /
path-preservation / integral-preservation closed under pointwise
operations).

\section{The operator norm \texorpdfstring{$\linhom\!$}{->hom\!}-\texorpdfstring{$\!\text{norm}$}{\!norm}}\label{sec:linhom-norm}

\begin{definition}[Operator norm on $C \lin D$, paper \S5.1]
\label{def:linhom-norm}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_norm}\rocqok\uses{def:linhom-car}
For $f \in C \lin D$,
\[
  \cnorm{f} := \sup\,\bigl\{\,\cnorm{f(x)} \;\big|\; x \in C,\;
  \cnorm{x} \le 1\,\bigr\}\;\in\;\R.
\]
The image set is non-empty (it contains $\cnorm{f(0)} = 0$) and
bounded above (any $\varepsilon$-witness of integrability of
$\varepsilon f$ yields the bound $\varepsilon^{-1}$ via M1's
\texttt{linmap\_norm}), so the supremum exists by the Dedekind
completeness of $\R$.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Why the canonical-sup formulation]
M1 originally exposed an operator norm extracted by
\texttt{xchoose} from the integrability witness
(\texttt{linmap\_norm}). That formulation is enough for the cone
axiom (Normh) on $\Cones(C, D)$, but it is the wrong shape for the
\emph{cone-on-the-internal-hom} (Normc) axiom: we need both the
upper-bound and the \emph{least}-upper-bound properties of the
norm at the same time, and we need them in a form that supports
$\omega$-continuity of the sup-of-chain operator on $C \lin D$.
The canonical real-valued sup form
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_norm} provides both via the lemmas
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_norm_sup_ub} (upper bound) and
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_norm_sup_lub} (least upper bound).
\end{remark}

\section{The diagonal-sup identity}\label{sec:linhom-diag-sup}

A structural lemma at the cone level, used heavily in the (Normc)
proof on $C \lin D$.

\begin{lemma}[Diagonal-sup identity]\label{lem:cone-sup-ball-addD}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.cone_sup_ball_addD}\rocqok\uses{def:cone}
Let $B$ be a cone (\autoref{ch:cones}) and let
$(a_n)_{n \in \N}$, $(b_n)_{n \in \N}$ be two $\preceq$-increasing
chains in the unit ball of $B$ whose diagonal sum
$(a_n + b_n)_{n \in \N}$ is itself $\preceq$-increasing and unit-ball.
Then
\[
  \bigsup_{n \in \N}\,(a_n + b_n)
  \;=\;
  \bigl(\bigsup_{n \in \N}\,a_n\bigr) + \bigl(\bigsup_{n \in \N}\,b_n\bigr)
\]
in $B$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The forward direction $\leq$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.cone_sup_ball_addD_le}) is immediate from
\texttt{cone\_sup\_ball\_lub}: pick at each level the witnesses
$z_a, z_b$ above $a_n, b_n$ in the two component sups; their sum is
above $a_n + b_n$ in the diagonal sup. The reverse direction
$\geq$ (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.cone_sup_ball_addD_ge}) is the
substantive half. Using $\max$ on the indices, every cross-term
$a_n + b_k$ is dominated by $a_m + b_m$ with $m = \max(n, k)$, hence
by the diagonal sup $S_s$, hence has norm $\le \cnorm{S_s} \le 1$.
Two applications of the scalar-distribution identities
\texttt{sup\_ball\_addr} / \texttt{sup\_ball\_addl} (from M1) then
reduce the bound on $S_a + S_b$ to a bound on a unit-ball chain of
cross-terms, which is again $\le S_s$. The two halves combine by
$\preceq$-antisymmetry.
\end{proof}

\section{The pointwise sup on \texorpdfstring{$C \lin D$}{C -> D}}\label{sec:linhom-sup-fun}

The cone-axiom (Normc) on $C \lin D$ requires a unit-ball chain
$(f_n)_{n}$ in the linhom-carrier to admit a pointwise supremum.
The construction proceeds in two steps.

\paragraph{On the unit ball.}
Given $x \in C$ with $\cnorm{x} \le 1$ and a chain
$(f_n)_n \subseteq C \lin D$ with $\cnorm{f_n} \le 1$, each
$f_n(x)$ lies in the unit ball of $D$, the chain $(f_n(x))_n$ is
$\preceq$-increasing (by pointwise monotonicity, see
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_le_pointwise}), and so admits a sup
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_unit} via $D$'s (Normc).

\paragraph{Extension by uniform scaling.}
For an arbitrary $x \in C$, define
$S := \cnorm{x} + 1 > 0$ and $x' := S^{-1} \cdot x$ (so
$\cnorm{x'} \le 1$). Set
\[
  \linhom\!\text{-}\sup\!{(f_n)}_n\,(x) \;:=\; S \cdot
  \bigsup_n\,f_n(x').
\]
This is \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_fun}. The factor
$\cnorm{x} + 1$ (rather than $\cnorm{x}$) avoids the degenerate case
$\cnorm{x} = 0$. The scaling lemma
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_fun_unitE} shows that this
extension agrees with \texttt{linhom\_sup\_unit} on the unit ball.

The body of \S5.1 then consists of verifying that the pointwise
sup inherits each \texttt{linhom\_car} field:
\begin{itemize}
  \item linearity at $0$, $+$, $\cdot$
    (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_fun_lin0},
    \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_fun_linD},
    \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_fun_linZ}) --- the additive
    case is the only delicate one; it uses
    \autoref{lem:cone-sup-ball-addD} at the common scaling factor
    $S = \cnorm{x} + \cnorm{y} + 1$;
  \item ball-boundedness with $M = 1$
    (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_fun_bounded});
  \item $\omega$-continuity
    (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_fun_continuous}) --- this
    relies on a sup-swap identity
    \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.cone_sup_ball_swap};
  \item path-preservation
    (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_fun_pres_path}) --- via a
    uniform-scale rewrite plus measurable-function convergence;
  \item integral-preservation
    (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_fun_pres_int}) --- the M3
    workhorse Lemma~4.7 (monotone convergence in $D$) combined with
    integral-preservation of each $f_n$.
\end{itemize}

The packaging is \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_ball}, a full
\texttt{linhom\_car}, with the norm bound
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_ball_norm}: $\cnorm{\sup_n f_n}
\le 1$.

\section{The difference helper \texorpdfstring{$\linhom\!\text{-diff}$}{->hom\!-diff}}\label{sec:linhom-diff}

The cone axiom (Normc) on $C \lin D$ requires \emph{both} that the
sup is an upper bound for the chain and that it is the least upper
bound. Both proofs share the same algebraic shape: given two
\texttt{linhom\_car}s $u, v$ with $u \preceq v$ pointwise (witnessed
per-$x$), exhibit the difference $v - u$ as a \texttt{linhom\_car}.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{linhom\_diff\_car}]\label{lem:linhom-diff}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_diff_car}\rocqok\uses{def:linhom-car}
Let $u, v : C \lin D$ with $\cnorm{v} \le 1$, and suppose that for
every $x \in C$ there exists $z \in D$ with $v(x) = u(x) + z$. Then
there exists a \texttt{linhom\_car} $\delta$ such that
$v(x) = u(x) + \delta(x)$ for all $x$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Define the underlying function $\delta(x)$ via classical choice
(\texttt{cid}) on the existential witness
(\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_diff_fun}). The defining equation
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_diff_E} reads
$v(x) = u(x) + \delta(x)$. The five \texttt{linhom\_car} fields then
follow by cancellation in $D$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item linearity, by cancelling $u$ against the linearity of $v$
  (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_diff_linear});
\item ball-boundedness, by $\delta(x) \preceq v(x)$ and the (Normp)
  axiom of $D$ (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_diff_bounded});
\item $\omega$-continuity, by $v$- and $u$-continuity on the chain
  and \autoref{lem:cone-sup-ball-addD} together with the (Cancel)
  axiom of $D$ (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_diff_continuous});
\item path-preservation, by $\delta(\gamma(r)) = v(\gamma(r)) -
  u(\gamma(r))$ pointwise and \texttt{measurable\_funB}
  (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_diff_pres_path});
\item integral-preservation, by integral-preservation of both $u$
  and $v$ and path-additivity of the Pettis integral
  (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_diff_pres_int}).
\end{enumerate}
The unit-norm hypothesis $\cnorm{v} \le 1$ is used by the
$\omega$-continuity step (to keep the chain $\delta(x_n)$ in the
unit ball).
\end{proof}

This helper is then re-used by \texttt{linhom\_sup\_ball\_ub} and
\texttt{linhom\_sup\_ball\_lub}, avoiding duplication of the five
field proofs.

\section{Cone structure on \texorpdfstring{$C \lin D$}{C -> D} (paper \S5.1)}%
\label{sec:linhom-cone}

\begin{lemma}[$C \lin D$ is a precone]\label{lem:linhom-precone}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_pre}\rocqok
\uses{def:linhom-car}
The pointwise zero, sum, and scalar action on \texttt{linhom\_car}
satisfy the precone axioms.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Each axiom (associativity, commutativity, zero, scalar
distribution, scalar associativity, scalar units, cancellation,
positivity) follows pointwise from the corresponding axiom of $D$,
via \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_eq} (extensionality of
\texttt{linhom\_car}). The HB instance is registered immediately
after \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_pos} (around line 1067 of
\texttt{linhom.v}).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[$C \lin D$ is a cone]\label{lem:linhom-cone}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_norm}\rocqok
\uses{lem:linhom-precone, lem:cone-sup-ball-addD, lem:linhom-diff}
With the canonical-sup operator norm of \autoref{def:linhom-norm},
the four norm axioms (Normh), (Normz), (Normt), (Normp) and the
$\omega$-completeness axiom (Normc) hold on $C \lin D$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
\begin{itemize}
\item (Normh) \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_normh}: scaling
  commutes with the sup over the unit ball.
\item (Normz) \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_normz}: if
  $\cnorm{f} = 0$ then $\cnorm{f(x)} = 0$ for every unit-ball $x$,
  hence $f(x) = 0$ by (Normz) of $D$; the uniform-scaling rewrite
  extends this to arbitrary $x$.
\item (Normt) \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_normt}: triangle
  inequality, pointwise.
\item (Normp) \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_normp}: monotonicity
  of the norm under $\preceq$, pointwise.
\item (Normc) is discharged by
  \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_ball_ub} (every $f_n$ is
  below the packaged sup, via \autoref{lem:linhom-diff}) and
  \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_ball_lub} (the sup is
  least, via the same helper after a uniform-scale rewrite to
  bring an arbitrary upper bound into the unit ball).
\end{itemize}
The \texttt{HB.instance Definition \_ := isCone.Build} registering
\texttt{linhom\_car Ar C D} as a \texttt{coneType R} appears at line
3247 of \texttt{linhom.v}. The sanity-check
\verb|Check (linhom_car Ar C D : coneType R)| follows.
\end{proof}

\section{Measurable-cone structure (paper Lemma 5.3)}%
\label{sec:linhom-mcone}

\begin{definition}[Test family $\gamma \triangleright m$, paper \S5.1
``Definition'' below Eq. (Mssep)]\label{def:linhom-test}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_test}\rocqok
\uses{lem:linhom-cone, def:path}
For $Y \in \Ar$, a measurable path
$\gamma \in \Path(Y, C)$ with $\cnorm{\gamma} \le 1$, and a test
$m \in \Mtest_Y^D$, define
\[
  (\gamma \triangleright m)(s, f)
  \;:=\; m\bigl(s,\,f(\gamma(s))\bigr),
  \qquad s \in Y,\ f \in C \lin D.
\]
The collection $\Mtest_Y^{C \lin D}$ is the set of all such
$\gamma \triangleright m$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Paper Lemma 5.3, $C \lin D$ is a measurable cone]
\label{lem:linhom-mcone}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_mcone_M}\rocqok
\uses{def:linhom-test, def:mcone}
The family $\{\Mtest_Y^{C \lin D}\}_{Y \in \Ar}$ satisfies the three
closure axioms (Mscomp), (Mssep), (Msnorm), so $C \lin D$ is a
measurable cone.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(Mscomp) \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_mcone_M_comp}: reindexing
a $\gamma \triangleright m$ by $\psi : Y' \to Y$ in $\Ar$ gives
$(\gamma \circ \psi) \triangleright (m \circ (\psi \times D))$,
which is again of the required form; the unit-ball constraint
$\cnorm{\gamma \circ \psi} \le \cnorm{\gamma} \le 1$ is preserved
because the image set of $\gamma \circ \psi$ is a subset of the
image of $\gamma$.

(Mssep) \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_mcone_M_sep}: at arity
$Y = 0$ the test family separates points: for each $x \in C$, take
the constant path $\gamma_{x'}$ at the rescaled
$x' := (\cnorm{x} + 1)^{-1} x \in B_C$ together with any
$m \in \Mtest_0^D$. If $(\gamma_{x'} \triangleright m)(f_1) =
(\gamma_{x'} \triangleright m)(f_2)$ for every such pair, then
linearity rescales back to $m(f_1(x)) = m(f_2(x))$ for every
$m \in \Mtest_0^D$, hence $f_1(x) = f_2(x)$ by (Mssep) in $D$.

(Msnorm) \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_mcone_M_norm}: the paper's
$\varepsilon/2 + \varepsilon/3$ argument
(\S5.1, just before ``So we have defined a measurable cone'').
Pick $x \in B_C$ with $\cnorm{f} \le \cnorm{f(x)} + \varepsilon/3$;
pick $m \in \Mtest_0^D \setminus \{0\}$ with
$\cnorm{f(x)} \le m(f(x))/\cnorm{m} + \min(\varepsilon/2,
\cnorm{f} - \varepsilon/2)$ by (Msnorm) in $D$; combine to
$\cnorm{f} \le (\gamma_x \triangleright m)(f) / \cnorm{\gamma_x
\triangleright m} + \varepsilon$.
\end{proof}

The HB instance \texttt{isMCone.Build} (line 3721 of
\texttt{linhom.v}) registers \texttt{linhom\_car Ar C D} as an
\texttt{mconeType Ar}; the sanity check
\verb|Check (linhom_car Ar C D : mconeType Ar)| follows.

\section{Integrable-cone structure (paper Lemma 5.4)}%
\label{sec:linhom-icone}

\begin{lemma}[Paper Lemma 5.4, $C \lin D$ is an integrable cone]
\label{lem:linhom-icone}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_exists}\rocqok
\uses{lem:linhom-mcone, def:icone}
For every $Y' \in \Ar$, every measurable path
$\eta \in \Path(Y', C \lin D)$, and every finite measure $\mu \in
\FMeas(Y')$, there exists a unique $\bar\eta \in C \lin D$
satisfying the Pettis equation w.r.t.\ the test family
$\Mtest^{C \lin D}$. Concretely,
\[
  \bar\eta(x) \;:=\; \Pettis{Y'}{} \eta(r)(x)\,\mu(\mathrm dr)
  \quad\in\; D,
\]
with the right-hand integral taken pointwise in the integrable
cone $D$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
Set $\bar\eta := \texttt{linhom\_int\_fun}$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_fun}). The five
\texttt{linhom\_car} fields are verified in turn:
\begin{itemize}
\item Pointwise measurability (each $r \mapsto \eta(r)(x)$ is a
  measurable path of $D$):
  \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_pt_meas}. The proof rescales
  $x$ to the unit ball via the
  $(\cnorm{x} + 1)$-scaling pattern of
  \autoref{sec:linhom-sup-fun}, then invokes (Msmeas) on $\eta$
  with the test $\gamma_{x'} \triangleright m_D$.
\item Boundedness (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_fun_bounded}):
  $\cnorm{\bar\eta(x)} \le \cnorm{\eta} \cdot \cnorm{\mu}$ on
  unit-ball $x$, by
  \texttt{path\_integral\_norm\_le} of M3.
\item Linearity (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_fun_linear}):
  the M3 bilinearity identities
  \texttt{path\_integral\_eq\_addB} and
  \texttt{path\_integral\_eq\_scaleB} on the integrand,
  combined with linearity of each $\eta(r)$.
\item $\omega$-continuity
  (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_fun_continuous}): monotone
  convergence in $D$ on the chain $r \mapsto \eta(r)(x_n)$,
  identifying $\sup_n \bar\eta(x_n)$ with $\bar\eta(\sup_n x_n)$.
\item Path-preservation
  (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_fun_pres_path}): for
  $\gamma \in \Path(X, C)$, the function $s \mapsto
  \bar\eta(\gamma(s))$ is a measurable path of $D$ by the joint
  test-measurability lemma
  \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_fun_joint_meas} combined with
  Lemma~4.7 of M3.
\item Integral-preservation
  (\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_fun_pres_int}): the
  fundamental \emph{Fubini} step. For a measurable path
  $\beta \in \Path(X, C)$ and a finite measure $\nu$ on $X$, both
  sides of
  $\bar\eta\bigl(\int \beta\,\mathrm d\nu\bigr) = \int
  \bar\eta(\beta(\cdot))\,\mathrm d\nu$ unfold to a double integral
  over $X \times Y'$ that agrees by cone-Fubini (M3, Theorem 4.15).
\end{itemize}
Finally, \rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_car_pettis} shows that
$\bar\eta$ satisfies the Pettis equation: for every test
$p = \gamma \triangleright m$ in $\Mtest_0^{C \lin D}$, the test
value $p(\bar\eta) = m(\bar\eta(\gamma(*)))$ equals the integral
$\int p(\eta(r))\,\mu(\mathrm dr)$, which is exactly the Pettis
equation in $C \lin D$.
\end{proof}

The HB instance \texttt{isICone.Build} (line 4563 of
\texttt{linhom.v}) registers \texttt{linhom\_car Ar C D} as an
\texttt{iconeType Ar}; the sanity check
\verb|Check (linhom_car Ar C D : iconeType Ar)| follows.

\section{Bilinear maps and the \texorpdfstring{$\lin$}{->} bijection (paper \S5.2)}%
\label{sec:bilin}

Paper \S5.2 (Def~5.6) defines the integrable cone of bilinear maps
formally as a curried internal hom:
\[
  C_1, C_2 \lin D \;:=\; C_1 \lin (C_2 \lin D).
\]
With \autoref{lem:linhom-icone} in place, the right-hand side is an
integrable cone in its own right. The paper then spells out, via its
remark using Lemma~2.19, the bijection at the function level
between elements of $C_1, C_2 \lin D$ and \emph{separately linear,
$\omega$-continuous, measurable, integrable} bilinear functions
$C_1 \& C_2 \to D$.

For the kernel-embedding anchor we deliver this bijection at the function level. A
\emph{bilinear datum}
(\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.bilin_data}) is a function $f : C_1 \to
C_2 \to D$ equipped with both a left-section
\texttt{linhom\_car} family $x_1 \mapsto f(x_1, \cdot)$ and a
right-section \texttt{linhom\_car} family
$x_2 \mapsto f(\cdot, x_2)$, together with two pointwise
agreement equations.

\begin{definition}[Currying / uncurrying, paper Def 5.6]
\label{def:bilin-linhom-bij}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.bilin_to_linhom}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_to_bilin}\rocqok
\uses{def:linhom-car}
\begin{itemize}
\item Curry: from a bilinear datum $f$, the function
  $x_1 \mapsto f(x_1, \cdot) : C_1 \to (C_2 \lin D)$ is
  \texttt{bilin\_to\_linhom}.
\item Uncurry: from a function $F : C_1 \to (C_2 \lin D)$ together
  with right-section data $H_{\mathrm{right}} : \forall x_2,
  \texttt{linhom\_car}\,C_1\,D$ matching $F$ pointwise, the
  function $(x_1, x_2) \mapsto F(x_1)(x_2) : C_1 \to C_2 \to D$
  packages as a bilinear datum via \texttt{linhom\_to\_bilin}.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Round-trip identities]\label{lem:bilin-roundtrip}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.bilin_to_linhom_E}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_to_bilin_E}\rocqok
\uses{def:bilin-linhom-bij}
For every bilinear datum $f$ and every pair $(x_1, x_2)$,
\[
  \mathtt{bilin\_to\_linhom}(f)(x_1)(x_2) \;=\; f(x_1, x_2),
\]
and dually for every $F : C_1 \to (C_2 \lin D)$ with right-section
data $H_{\mathrm{right}}$,
\[
  \mathtt{linhom\_to\_bilin}(F, H_{\mathrm{right}})(x_1, x_2)
  \;=\; F(x_1)(x_2).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The first identity is the pointwise agreement field
\texttt{bilin\_left\_eq} of \texttt{bilin\_data}. The second
identity is definitional (the uncurry is
$(x_1, x_2) \mapsto F(x_1)(x_2)$). Both proofs close by
\texttt{erefl} / \texttt{exact}.
\end{proof}

These function-level round-trips are exactly the data needed by
\autoref{ch:skern} (the dirac round-trip in the
faithfulness/fullness proof of paper Theorem~6.5). The morphism-level
upgrade to an iso in $\ICones$ uses the full HB tower on
\texttt{linhom\_car} delivered in \autoref{sec:linhom-icone}; the
explicit categorical statement of paper Theorem~5.12
($(B \otimes C) \lin D \simeq B \lin (C \lin D)$) is part of the tensor
chapter (\autoref{thm:thm512}).

\section{Design choices}\label{sec:linhom-design}

\paragraph{Concrete internal hom vs SAFT.}
Paper \S5 sketches two routes to the cartesian closed structure on
$\ICones$: the concrete construction of $C \lin D$ as a set of
integrable linear maps with a hand-built measurability structure
(paper \S5.1), and the abstract construction via SAFT applied to
$C \lin -\;$. The internal hom $\lin$ is built concretely in
\texttt{theories/homs/linhom.v}; the tensor product $\otimes$ and the
exponential $\mathord{!}$, which the paper builds via the abstract
route, are constructed by the concrete M-SAFT machinery of
\texttt{theories/icones/representable.v} in Chapters~\ref{ch:tensor}
and~\ref{ch:exponential}.

\paragraph{Canonical-sup norm.}
M1's \texttt{linmap\_norm} extracts an upper bound on
$\{\cnorm{f(x)} \mid \cnorm{x} \le 1\}$ via \texttt{xchoose}; this
is enough for the cone axioms on $\Cones(C, D)$ but not for the
(Normc) axiom on the internal hom $C \lin D$, which needs the
least-upper-bound property. The canonical-sup form
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_norm} provides both an upper
bound (\texttt{linhom\_norm\_sup\_ub}) and a least-upper-bound
property (\texttt{linhom\_norm\_sup\_lub}).

\paragraph{The \texorpdfstring{$(\cnorm{x} + 1)$}{(x + 1)}-scaling pattern.}
Many proofs about $C \lin D$ first reduce an arbitrary $x \in C$
to a unit-ball element $x' := (\cnorm{x} + 1)^{-1} x$, do the
work on the unit ball, and rescale back by $S := \cnorm{x} + 1$.
The $+1$ avoids the degenerate case $\cnorm{x} = 0$ (where
$\cnorm{x}^{-1}$ would not exist). This pattern appears in
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_sup_fun} (Step 1 of (Normc)),
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_mcone_M_sep} ((Mssep)),
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_int_pt_meas} (pointwise
measurability of the integral), and several path-preservation /
joint-measurability lemmas in the same file.

\paragraph{The difference helper.}
\rocq{Icones.homs.linhom.linhom_diff_car} packages the
``$v - u$'' \texttt{linhom\_car} once, exposing the five field
proofs as separate cancellation arguments. Without it, both
\texttt{linhom\_sup\_ball\_ub} and \texttt{linhom\_sup\_ball\_lub}
would have to repeat the same five proofs.

\paragraph{The four-layer HB tower on \texttt{linhom\_car}.}
The whole construction climbs the four layers
$\PCone \to \Cone \to \MCone \to \ICone$ on the same carrier
\texttt{linhom\_car Ar C D}. Each layer is registered by an
\texttt{HB.instance Definition} at the appropriate line of
\texttt{linhom.v}:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{isPrecone.Build} at line 1067 --- algebraic structure
  is pointwise;
\item \texttt{isCone.Build} at line 3247 --- canonical-sup norm +
  diagonal-sup identity + the difference helper;
\item \texttt{isMCone.Build} at line 3721 --- the test family
  $\gamma \triangleright m$ and the three closure axioms;
\item \texttt{isICone.Build} at line 4563 --- the pointwise Pettis
  integral, with the Fubini step for integral-preservation.
\end{itemize}
The sanity checks
\verb|linhom_car Ar C D : coneType R|,
\verb|: mconeType Ar|, and
\verb|: iconeType Ar|
all type-check in the same file (\texttt{LinhomConeCheck},
\texttt{LinhomMConeCheck}, \texttt{LinhomIConeCheck} sections).
