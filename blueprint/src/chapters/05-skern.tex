\chapter{Substochastic kernels and the MVP theorem}\label{ch:skern}

This chapter formalises paper \S6 and the MVP money theorem:
substochastic kernels on $\Ar$-objects form a category $\Skern$, and
the obvious assignment $X \mapsto \FMeas(X)$,
$\kappa \mapsto \lambda \mu.\,\mu \otimes \kappa$ extends to a fully
faithful functor $\Skern \hookrightarrow \ICones$ (paper
Theorem~6.5). All material in this chapter lives under
\texttt{theories/kernels/}.
