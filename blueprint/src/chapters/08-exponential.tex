\chapter{The exponential and the Seely category}\label{ch:exponential}

This chapter formalises paper \S9: the exponential modality
$\mathord{!}$ of intuitionistic linear logic on $\ICones$, the
linear/non-linear adjunction relating $\ICones$ to the cartesian closed
category $\mathbf{SCones}$ of Chapter~\ref{ch:stable}, and the
\emph{Seely category} structure (in the sense of
Melli\`es~\cite{mellies-categorical-semantics}) that makes $\ICones$ a
model of intuitionistic linear logic. It is the direct continuation of
the cartesian closed structure of $\mathbf{SCones}$
(\autoref{thm:scones-ccc}) and the dereliction inclusion $\mathrm{Der}$
(\autoref{lem:ders}).

The construction is the classical one. The dereliction
$\mathrm{Der} : \ICones \to \mathbf{SCones}$ (\autoref{lem:ders}) is a
limit-preserving functor between locally presentable, well-powered
categories; Freyd's Special Adjoint Functor Theorem (SAFT) therefore
produces a left adjoint $E \dashv \mathrm{Der}$, with no concrete
description of $E$. The induced comonad $\mathord{!} := E \circ
\mathrm{Der}$ on $\ICones$ has co-Kleisli category equivalent to
$\mathbf{SCones}$ (the linear/non-linear adjunction of
Melli\`es~\cite{mellies-categorical-semantics}), and the strong
monoidal comonad structure of $\mathord{!}$ against the tensor of
Chapter~\ref{ch:tensor} exhibits $\ICones$ as a Seely category --- the
categorical model of the $\mathord{!}$ modality. Crucially, the
\emph{same} Special Adjoint Functor Theorem underlies both the tensor
$\otimes$ (\autoref{ch:tensor}) and the exponential $\mathord{!}$: $E$
is the left adjoint of the limit-preserving $\mathrm{Der}$, exactly as
$\otimes$ is obtained from the limit-preserving internal hom $C \lin -$.

The material lives under \texttt{theories/homs/bang\_construct.v} (the
$E \dashv \mathrm{Der}$ SAFT construction, now proved),
\texttt{theories/homs/bang.v} (the comonad $\mathord{!}$) and
\texttt{theories/homs/seely.v} (the Seely category). The adjunction is
discharged in place in \texttt{theories/axioms/exp\_interface.v} (its
\texttt{Parameter}s are now \texttt{Definition}s/\texttt{Lemma}s backed
by \texttt{bang\_construct}); the Seely isomorphisms remain the project's
single staging interface, \texttt{theories/axioms/seely\_interface.v}.

\section{The $E \dashv \mathrm{Der}$ adjunction (paper \S9), discharged}%
\label{sec:exp-staging}

The deep input to the exponential is the \emph{existence} of the left
adjoint $E$ of $\mathrm{Der}$, which the paper obtains from SAFT and for
which it gives \emph{no} concrete carrier --- the explicit parallel of
Remark~5.1 for the tensor. The Rocq development originally \emph{staged}
this content (a minimal \emph{universal-arrow} interface in
\texttt{theories/axioms/exp\_interface.v}); it is now \textbf{discharged}
--- proved axiom-free --- so the $\mathord{!}$ comonad depends only on
the classical base. The discharge mirrors the tensor's T1, instantiated
with $R := \mathrm{Der}$: \texttt{theories/stable/der\_continuous.v}
proves $\mathrm{Der}$ preserves all limits (\textbf{Theorem 7.34}), so
the Special Adjoint Functor Theorem produces $E$, which
\texttt{theories/homs/bang\_construct.v} constructs concretely as a wide
intersection on a coseparator power (the same SAFT machinery as
\texttt{tensor\_construct.v}). The five declarations of
\texttt{exp\_interface.v} are now \texttt{Definition}s/\texttt{Lemma}s
backed by \texttt{bang\_construct}; they are \emph{retained in place}
(not deleted) because the still-staged Seely development
(\texttt{seely.v}, \texttt{seely\_interface.v}) imports
$\mathord{!}$/$\mathrm{nl}$ and must share the same $\mathord{!}$ ---
\texttt{Strategy opaque} + \texttt{Arguments \ldots : simpl never}
reproduce the former \texttt{Parameter} irreducibility so those
consumers compile unchanged. There is no \Admitted{} anywhere, and
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.Bang\_comonad} is axiom-clean.

\begin{theorem}[$\mathrm{Der}$ preserves all limits, paper Theorem 7.34]
\label{thm:der-continuous}
\rocq{Icones.stable.der\_continuous.der\_preserves\_limits}\rocqok
\uses{lem:ders, thm:scones-prod}
$\mathrm{Der} : \ICones \to \mathbf{SCones}$ preserves products --- the
$\mathbf{SCones}$ product is the same $\ICones$ product
($\texttt{scones\_proj}\,i = \mathrm{Der}(\texttt{icones\_proj}\,i)$) ---
and equalisers: a stable map equalising $\mathrm{Der}\,f$, $\mathrm{Der}\,g$
co-restricts to the $\ICones$-equaliser, since $\mathrm{Der}$ is the
identity on underlying functions. This is the easy analogue, for the
dereliction, of Theorem 5.9 (\autoref{thm:scones-ccc} prerequisites).
\end{theorem}

The adjunction is presented by the natural bijection
\[
  \Theta : \ICones(\mathord{!}B, C) \;\simeq\; \mathbf{SCones}(B, C),
  \qquad
  \Theta\,h \;:=\; \mathrm{Der}(h) \circ \mathrm{nl}_B,
\]
whose five universal-arrow components are now proved (no longer
\notready):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{The exponential object former} $\mathord{!}B = E\,B$
    (paper $E$).\label{def:bang-object}
    \rocq{Icones.axioms.exp\_interface.Bang}\rocqok{} --- now the
    concrete wide intersection of \texttt{bang\_construct}, not an
    opaque carrier.
  \item \emph{The unit / universal nonlinear map} (paper
    $\textsf{Unistab}_B$). \rocq{Icones.axioms.exp\_interface.nl}\rocqok{}
    --- the unit $\eta_B = \mathrm{nl}_B : B \to \mathord{!}B$, a
    $\mathbf{SCones}$-morphism.
  \item \emph{The linear factoriser} (paper $\Theta^{-1}$).
    \rocq{Icones.axioms.exp\_interface.lin}\rocqok{} --- the SAFT
    mediator, built like $\texttt{tensor\_uncurry}$.
  \item \emph{The existence half} of the universal property.
    \rocq{Icones.axioms.exp\_interface.lin\_beta}\rocqok{} ---
    $\mathrm{Der}(\mathrm{lin}\,f) \circ \mathrm{nl}_B = f$.
  \item \emph{The uniqueness half} of the universal property.
    \rocq{Icones.axioms.exp\_interface.lin\_unique}\rocqok{} ---
    proved by the equaliser-of-candidates + intersection-minimality
    argument (like $\texttt{tensor\_curry\_inj}$). With \texttt{lin\_beta}
    this makes $\Theta$ and $\mathrm{lin}$ mutually inverse.
\end{itemize}

\section{The exponential comonad $\mathord{!}$ (paper \S9)}%
\label{sec:comonad}

Since the $E \dashv \mathrm{Der}$ adjunction is now discharged
(\S\ref{sec:exp-staging}), everything in this section is axiom-free:
the comonad and all its laws depend only on the classical base
(\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.Bang\_comonad} is axiom-clean). We write
$\mathord{!}B := \texttt{Bang}\,\Ar\,B$.

\subsection{The hom-bijection $\Theta$ and promotion}\label{sec:theta}

\begin{definition}[The hom-bijection $\Theta$, paper \S9]\label{def:theta}
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.Theta}\rocqok\uses{lem:ders, sec:exp-staging}
The forward map of the adjunction $\Theta : \ICones(\mathord{!}B, C)
\to \mathbf{SCones}(B, C)$ sends a linear $h$ to the stable map
$\Theta\,h := \mathrm{Der}(h) \circ \mathrm{nl}_B$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.Theta}, where $\mathrm{Der} = \texttt{ders}$).
It is inverse to the factoriser $\mathrm{lin}$: $\Theta(\mathrm{lin}\,f)
= f$ (\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.ThetaK}, directly
\rocq{Icones.axioms.exp\_interface.lin\_beta}) and
$\mathrm{lin}(\Theta\,h) = h$ (\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.linK}, the
uniqueness \rocq{Icones.axioms.exp\_interface.lin\_unique} with
$f := \Theta\,h$).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Promotion $x!$, paper $\textsf{Prom}\,x$]\label{def:prom}
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.prom}\rocqok\uses{sec:exp-staging}
The \emph{promoted point} of $x \in B$ is $x! := \mathrm{nl}_B(x) \in
\mathord{!}B$ (\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.prom}). Since the unit
$\mathrm{nl}_B$ is a $\mathbf{SCones}$-morphism, hence norm-$\leq 1$,
the promotion of a unit-ball point is again in the unit ball:
$\cnorm{x} \leq 1 \Rightarrow \cnorm{x!} \leq 1$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.prom\_ball}).
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Pointwise reading of $\Theta$ on the unit ball]\label{lem:theta-prom}
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.Theta\_prom}\rocqok\uses{def:theta, def:prom}
For a linear $h : \mathord{!}B \to C$ and $\cnorm{x} \leq 1$,
$(\Theta\,h)(x) = h(x!)$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Unfold $\Theta = \mathrm{Der}(h) \circ \mathrm{nl}_B$ on the ball: the
outer $0$-extension clamp of $\texttt{scones\_comp}$ is transparent
there ($\texttt{sc\_clamp\_ball}$), and $\mathrm{Der}(h)$ applied to
$x!$ is its clamp of the underlying linear function, transparent again
since $x!$ is in the ball by \autoref{def:prom}.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The extensionality principle (paper Lemma
\texttt{tens-excl-equal-charact}, $n = 1$)}\label{sec:bang-ext}

This is the workhorse of the whole chapter: a linear map out of
$\mathord{!}B$ is determined by its values on promoted points. It is
the paper's proof method for \emph{every} comonad and Seely law ---
reduce to promoted points and compute.

\begin{lemma}[Promotion extensionality, paper Lemma
\texttt{tens-excl-equal-charact} ($n = 1$)]\label{lem:bang-ext}
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.bang\_ext}\rocqok\uses{def:theta, lem:theta-prom}
Two linear maps $f, g : \mathord{!}B \to C$ that agree on every promoted
point $x!$ (for $\cnorm{x} \leq 1$) are equal.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By the bijection it suffices that $\Theta\,f = \Theta\,g$, since then
$f = \mathrm{lin}(\Theta\,f) = \mathrm{lin}(\Theta\,g) = g$ by
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.linK}. Equality of the two $\mathbf{SCones}$-morphisms
is checked by $\texttt{scones\_hom\_eq}$: on the unit ball
$(\Theta\,f)(x) = f(x!) = g(x!) = (\Theta\,g)(x)$ by
\autoref{lem:theta-prom} and the hypothesis; off the ball both are $0$
by the $\texttt{scones\_comp}$ $0$-extension.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Design note: every law on promoted points.}
The pattern \autoref{lem:bang-ext} institutes --- and which every law
below follows --- is exactly the paper's method: to prove an equation
between linear maps out of $\mathord{!}B$, apply \texttt{bang\_ext} and
compute both sides on a promoted point $x!$, where $\mathrm{der}$,
$\mathrm{dig}$ and $\mathord{!}f$ all have transparent values
(\autoref{def:struct-maps}). This is the exact analogue, for the
$\mathord{!}$-comonad, of the pure-tensor extensionality
\autoref{lem:tensor-ext} (Prop~5.14) that makes all monoidal coherence
provable in Chapter~\ref{ch:tensor}.

\subsection{Functoriality, counit and comultiplication}\label{sec:struct-maps}

\begin{definition}[The structure maps of $\mathord{!}$, paper \S9]\label{def:struct-maps}
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.bang\_fmap}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.der}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.dig}\rocqok
\uses{def:theta}
The functorial action of $\mathord{!}$ on a morphism $f : B \to C$ is
$\mathord{!}f := \mathrm{lin}(\mathrm{nl}_C \circ \mathrm{Der}(f)) =
E(\mathrm{Der}\,f) : \mathord{!}B \to \mathord{!}C$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.bang\_fmap}). The counit (dereliction) is
$\mathrm{der}_B := \mathrm{lin}(\id_B) : \mathord{!}B \to B$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.der}), and the comultiplication (digging) is
$\mathrm{dig}_B := \mathrm{lin}(\mathrm{nl}_{\mathord{!}B} \circ
\mathrm{nl}_B) = E(\mathrm{nl}_B) : \mathord{!}B \to \mathord{!}\mathord{!}B$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.dig}).
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Computation on promoted points, paper Lemma
\texttt{excl-fun-prom}]\label{lem:struct-prom}
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.bang\_fmap\_prom}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.der\_prom}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.dig\_prom}\rocqok
\uses{def:struct-maps, lem:theta-prom}
For $\cnorm{x} \leq 1$, the three structure maps send a promoted point
to a promoted point:
\[
  (\mathord{!}f)(x!) = (f\,x)!,
  \qquad
  \mathrm{der}_B(x!) = x,
  \qquad
  \mathrm{dig}_B(x!) = (x!)! = x!!.
\]
namely \rocq{Icones.homs.bang.bang\_fmap\_prom} (paper Lemma
\texttt{excl-fun-prom}), \rocq{Icones.homs.bang.der\_prom} and
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.dig\_prom}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Each is the corresponding case of $\Theta(\mathord{!}f) = \mathrm{nl}_C
\circ \mathrm{Der}(f)$, $\Theta(\mathrm{der}_B) = \id_B$,
$\Theta(\mathrm{dig}_B) = \mathrm{nl}_{\mathord{!}B} \circ \mathrm{nl}_B$
(by \rocq{Icones.homs.bang.ThetaK}), evaluated at $x$ through
\autoref{lem:theta-prom} and unfolded on the ball. For
\texttt{bang\_fmap\_prom}, $\mathrm{Der}(f)(x) = f\,x$ is in the ball
since $f$ is norm-$\leq 1$, and $\mathrm{nl}_C$ of it is $(f\,x)!$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Functor laws]\label{lem:bang-functor}
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.bang\_fmap\_id}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.bang\_fmap\_comp}\rocqok
\uses{lem:bang-ext, lem:struct-prom}
$\mathord{!}$ preserves identities, $\mathord{!}(\id_B) =
\id_{\mathord{!}B}$ (\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.bang\_fmap\_id}), and
composition, $\mathord{!}(g \circ f) = \mathord{!}g \circ \mathord{!}f$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.bang\_fmap\_comp}).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Both by \autoref{lem:bang-ext}, reducing to promoted points: on $x!$,
$\mathord{!}(\id)(x!) = (\id\,x)! = x!$ and $\mathord{!}(g \circ f)(x!)
= (g(f\,x))! = \mathord{!}g\bigl((f\,x)!\bigr) = (\mathord{!}g \circ
\mathord{!}f)(x!)$, by repeated \autoref{lem:struct-prom} (the
composition step uses that $f\,x$ stays in the ball, $\texttt{ders\_lin\_ball}$).
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Naturality of $\mathrm{der}$ and $\mathrm{dig}$]\label{lem:der-dig-nat}
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.der\_nat}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.dig\_nat}\rocqok
\uses{def:struct-maps, lem:struct-prom}
The counit is natural, $f \circ \mathrm{der}_B = \mathrm{der}_C \circ
\mathord{!}f$ (\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.der\_nat}), and the
comultiplication is natural, $\mathord{!}\mathord{!}f \circ
\mathrm{dig}_B = \mathrm{dig}_C \circ \mathord{!}f$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.dig\_nat}).
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By \autoref{lem:bang-ext}: on $x!$ both sides of the counit square give
$f\,x$, and both sides of the comultiplication square give
$((f\,x)!)!$, each by \autoref{lem:struct-prom}.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[$(\mathord{!}, \mathrm{der}, \mathrm{dig})$ is a
comonad, paper \S9]\label{thm:comonad}
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.comonad\_counitL}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.comonad\_counitR}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.comonad\_coassoc}\rocqok
\uses{lem:bang-functor, lem:der-dig-nat, lem:struct-prom}
The triple $(\mathord{!}, \mathrm{der}, \mathrm{dig})$ satisfies the
comonad laws: the two counit laws
\[
  \mathrm{der}_{\mathord{!}B} \circ \mathrm{dig}_B = \id_{\mathord{!}B},
  \qquad
  \mathord{!}(\mathrm{der}_B) \circ \mathrm{dig}_B = \id_{\mathord{!}B},
\]
(\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.comonad\_counitL},
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.comonad\_counitR}) and coassociativity
\[
  \mathrm{dig}_{\mathord{!}B} \circ \mathrm{dig}_B
  = \mathord{!}(\mathrm{dig}_B) \circ \mathrm{dig}_B
\]
(\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.comonad\_coassoc}).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Each by \autoref{lem:bang-ext}, reduced to a promoted point $x!$ via
\autoref{lem:struct-prom}. Left counit:
$\mathrm{der}_{\mathord{!}B}(\mathrm{dig}_B(x!)) =
\mathrm{der}_{\mathord{!}B}(x!!) = x!$. Right counit:
$\mathord{!}(\mathrm{der}_B)(x!!) = (\mathrm{der}_B(x!))! = x!$.
Coassociativity: both composites send $x!$ to $((x!)!)! = x!!!$.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[The comonad record and its witness]\label{def:comonad-record}
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.Comonad}\rocqok
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.Bang\_comonad}\rocqok
\uses{thm:comonad}
The comonad structure is bundled in the record
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.Comonad}, packaging the object map, the
functorial action, the counit and the comultiplication together with
the five laws (functor identity/composition, the two counit laws,
coassociativity) --- mirroring \rocq{Icones.homs.smcc.ICones\_SMCC} of
Chapter~\ref{ch:tensor}. The canonical witness
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.Bang\_comonad} populates every field with the
proved lemmas of \autoref{lem:bang-functor} and \autoref{thm:comonad}.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[The linear/non-linear adjunction]
The bijection $\Theta$ (\autoref{def:theta}) is precisely the
co-Kleisli equivalence: morphisms $\mathord{!}B \to C$ in $\ICones$
correspond to stable maps $B \to C$ in $\mathbf{SCones}$. Thus the
co-Kleisli category of $\mathord{!}$ is (equivalent to)
$\mathbf{SCones}$, and $E \dashv \mathrm{Der}$ is the linear/non-linear
adjunction of Melli\`es~\cite{mellies-categorical-semantics} ---
exhibiting $\ICones$ (linear) and $\mathbf{SCones}$ (non-linear,
cartesian closed by \autoref{thm:scones-ccc}) as a model of
intuitionistic linear logic.
\end{remark}

\section{The Seely category (paper \S9)}\label{sec:seely}

To make $\ICones$ a Seely category we must exhibit $\mathord{!}$ as a
\emph{strong monoidal} comonad from the cartesian product
$(\mathbin{\&}, \top)$ of $\mathbf{SCones}$/$\ICones$ to the tensor
$(\otimes, 1)$ of Chapter~\ref{ch:tensor}: the \emph{Seely
isomorphisms}
\[
  \mathrm{Seely2}_{B_1,B_2} : \mathord{!}B_1 \otimes \mathord{!}B_2
  \;\cong\; \mathord{!}(B_1 \mathbin{\&} B_2),
  \qquad
  \mathrm{Seely0} : 1 \;\cong\; \mathord{!}\top,
\]
natural and coherent with the monoidal and cartesian structure. The
tensor (\autoref{sec:tensor-staging}) and the exponential
(\S\ref{sec:exp-staging}) are both discharged, so this section works
modulo the \emph{single} remaining interface --- the Seely-iso existence
of \texttt{theories/axioms/seely\_interface.v}; the coherence
\emph{laws} are then genuinely proved.

\subsection{The $\mathord{!}$-tensor extensionality}\label{sec:tens-excl}

The lever for the Seely category --- the exact analogue, for the
$\mathord{!}$-tensor, of the pure-tensor extensionality
\autoref{lem:tensor-ext} (Prop~5.14) --- is that a linear map out of
$\mathord{!}B_1 \otimes \mathord{!}B_2$ is determined by its values on
\emph{promoted} pure tensors $x_1! \otimes x_2!$. Its proof needs a
bridge from the inner curried image (a \texttt{linhom\_car} element)
back to a morphism.

\begin{definition}[Element$\to$morphism bridge, paper \S5.1]\label{def:linhom-icones}
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.linhom\_icones}\rocqok\uses{def:linhom-car}
A \texttt{linhom\_car}\,$\Ar\,C\,D$ element $\varphi$ of operator norm
$\leq 1$ is exactly the data of an
$\icones_{\mathrm{hom}}\,\Ar\,C\,D$: linearity, $\omega$-continuity,
path- and integral-preservation come verbatim from $\varphi$, and the
only extra requirement of $\cones_{\mathrm{hom}}$ --- the per-point
bound $\cnorm{\varphi\,x} \leq \cnorm{x}$ --- is
\texttt{linhom\_norm\_apply\_le} at $K = 1$. The packaging is
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.linhom\_icones}, with computation law
$\texttt{linhom\_icones}\,\varphi\,x = \varphi\,x$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.linhom\_iconesE}).
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[$n = 1$ extensionality at the \texttt{linhom\_car} level]\label{lem:bang-ext-linhom}
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.bang\_ext\_linhom}\rocqok
\uses{lem:bang-ext, def:linhom-icones}
Two norm-$\leq 1$ linear maps $\varphi, \psi : \mathord{!}B \lin C$
agreeing on every promoted point $x!$ (for $\cnorm{x} \leq 1$) are
equal. Package each as an $\icones_{\mathrm{hom}}$ via
\autoref{def:linhom-icones}, discharge by the $n = 1$
\autoref{lem:bang-ext}, and read back through $\texttt{linhom\_eq}$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Paper Lemma \texttt{tens-excl-equal-charact}, $n = 2$]\label{lem:tens-excl-charact}
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.tens\_excl\_charact}\rocqok
\uses{lem:bang-ext, lem:bang-ext-linhom, thm:thm512, lem:eq51}
Two linear maps $f, g : \mathord{!}B_1 \otimes \mathord{!}B_2 \to C$
that agree on every promoted pure tensor $x_1! \otimes x_2!$ (for
$\cnorm{x_1} \leq 1$, $\cnorm{x_2} \leq 1$) are equal. This is the
$\mathord{!}$-tensor promotion extensionality --- THE workhorse for the
Seely coherence.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
The paper's $n = 2$ induction, unfolded. Curry $f, g$ to $F = \Phi(f),
G = \Phi(g) : \mathord{!}B_1 \to (\mathord{!}B_2 \lin C)$ with the
(now proved) tensor adjunction; by injectivity of $\Phi$
(\rocq{Icones.homs.tensor.tensor\_curry\_inj}, \autoref{thm:thm512}) it
suffices that $F = G$. By the $n = 1$ \autoref{lem:bang-ext} it suffices
that $F(x_1!) = G(x_1!) : \mathord{!}B_2 \lin C$ for $\cnorm{x_1} \leq
1$. These are \texttt{linhom\_car} elements of norm $\leq 1$ (image of
the norm-$\leq 1$ morphism $F$ at the unit-ball point $x_1!$,
\autoref{def:prom}); by the \texttt{linhom}-level
\autoref{lem:bang-ext-linhom} it suffices they agree on every $x_2!$,
i.e.\ $F(x_1!)(x_2!) = G(x_1!)(x_2!)$. By Eq~5.1
(\rocq{Icones.homs.tensor.tensor\_curryE}, \autoref{lem:eq51}) this is
$f(x_1! \otimes x_2!) = g(x_1! \otimes x_2!)$, the hypothesis.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The ternary case]
The $n = 3$ extensionality (maps out of $\mathord{!}B_1 \otimes
\mathord{!}B_2 \otimes \mathord{!}B_3$ determined on $x_1! \otimes x_2!
\otimes x_3!$, \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.tens\_excl\_charact3}\rocqok{}) is
proved by exactly one more currying layer, on the model of the ternary
tensor extensionality \autoref{lem:tensor-ext3}; it feeds the
associativity Seely-coherence square.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The staged Seely isomorphisms}\label{sec:seely-isos}

The tensor's structural isos $\alpha/\lambda/\rho/\sigma$

but the \emph{existence} of the Seely isos --- the remaining Yoneda
content --- is still \emph{staged}, and we \emph{derive} the coherence
from it. The isos and their characterising equations on promoted pure
tensors are collected in \texttt{theories/axioms/seely\_interface.v},
each flagged \texttt{STAGING: discharge via M-SAFT + Yoneda, PLAN
\S13.4; then delete}. This is the project's \emph{single} remaining
staged interface. The concrete discharge plan is \textbf{Lemma~9.4}
($B \Rightarrow (C \lin D) \cong C \lin (B \Rightarrow D)$, natural ---
the largest remaining piece) together with the unit point-bijection
$\mathbf{SCones}(\top, C) \simeq \ICones(1, C)$; the contravariant
Yoneda assembly tool \rocq{Icones.homs.icones\_iso.co\_yoneda\_iso} is
already merged.

\begin{definition}[The binary Seely iso, paper \S9]\label{def:seely2}
\rocq{Icones.axioms.seely\_interface.Seely2}\notready{}
\rocq{Icones.axioms.seely\_interface.Seely2E}\notready{}
\uses{def:icones-iso, def:prom, sec:exp-staging}
The binary Seely isomorphism is the \texttt{icones\_iso}
\[
  \mathrm{Seely2}_{B_1,B_2} : \mathord{!}B_1 \otimes \mathord{!}B_2
  \;\cong\; \mathord{!}(B_1 \mathbin{\&} B_2)
\]
(\rocq{Icones.axioms.seely\_interface.Seely2}), staged together with its
characterising equation on promoted pure tensors (paper's explicit
formula): for $\cnorm{x_1} \leq 1$, $\cnorm{x_2} \leq 1$,
\[
  \mathrm{Seely2}(x_1! \otimes x_2!) = \langle x_1, x_2 \rangle!
\]
(\rocq{Icones.axioms.seely\_interface.Seely2E}, where $\langle x_1, x_2
\rangle = \texttt{sprod\_pair}\,x_1\,x_2$). By
\autoref{lem:tens-excl-charact} this single equation \emph{fully
determines} $\mathrm{Seely2}$. The staged interface also supplies the
naturality of $\mathrm{Seely2}$ in both slots
(\rocq{Icones.axioms.seely\_interface.Seely2\_natural}, the square
$\mathord{!}(f_1 \mathbin{\&} f_2) \circ \mathrm{Seely2} = \mathrm{Seely2}
\circ (\mathord{!}f_1 \otimes \mathord{!}f_2)$, with the binary-product
bifunctor action \rocq{Icones.axioms.seely\_interface.sprod\_mor}), the
Yoneda output.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[The unit Seely iso $\mathrm{Seely0}$]
The unit Seely iso $\mathrm{Seely0} : 1 \cong \mathord{!}\top$ (paper's

\end{remark}

\subsection{The derived Seely coherence}\label{sec:seely-coherence}

As in \autoref{sec:coherence} for the tensor, the point of
\autoref{lem:tens-excl-charact} is that, although the Seely \emph{isos}
are staged, every Seely-category coherence \emph{law} is genuinely
\emph{proved}, by checking the two sides agree on promoted pure tensors
$x_1! \otimes x_2!$ and reading off the computation laws
($\mathrm{Seely2E}$, $\mathrm{dig\_prom}$, $\mathrm{bang\_fmap\_prom}$,
$\texttt{tensor\_morE}$) together with the projection/tuple laws of
\texttt{scones\_ccc.v}.

The worked example delivered in full is the comultiplication coherence
the paper draws explicitly. First the transport tuple.

\begin{definition}[The $\mathbin{\&}$-projection tuple]\label{def:bang-proj-tuple}
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.bang\_proj\_tuple}\rocqok
\uses{lem:bang-functor, thm:scones-prod}
The tuple $\langle \mathord{!}\pi_1, \mathord{!}\pi_2 \rangle :
\mathord{!}(B_1 \mathbin{\&} B_2) \to (\mathord{!}B_1 \mathbin{\&}
\mathord{!}B_2)$ (paper's $\langle \mathord{!}\mathrm{Proj}_1,
\mathord{!}\mathrm{Proj}_2 \rangle$) is
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.bang\_proj\_tuple}, built from the
$\mathbin{\&}$-projections \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.sproj1} /
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.sproj2} via \texttt{icones\_tuple}; its
pairing law is \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.bang\_proj\_tupleE}.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Comultiplication coherence, paper \S9]\label{lem:seely-comult}
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_comult}\rocqok
\uses{lem:tens-excl-charact, def:seely2, lem:struct-prom, def:bang-proj-tuple}
Transporting the comultiplication $\mathrm{dig}$ across
$\mathrm{Seely2}$ commutes with the $\mathbin{\&}$-projection tuple, as
maps $\mathord{!}B_1 \otimes \mathord{!}B_2 \to \mathord{!}(\mathord{!}B_1
\mathbin{\&} \mathord{!}B_2)$:
\[
  \mathord{!}\langle \mathord{!}\pi_1, \mathord{!}\pi_2 \rangle \circ
  \mathrm{dig}_{B_1 \mathbin{\&} B_2} \circ \mathrm{Seely2}_{B_1,B_2}
  \;=\;
  \mathrm{Seely2}_{\mathord{!}B_1,\mathord{!}B_2} \circ
  (\mathrm{dig}_{B_1} \otimes \mathrm{dig}_{B_2}).
\]
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
By \autoref{lem:tens-excl-charact} it suffices the two sides agree on
$x_1! \otimes x_2!$. The right-hand side reduces, through the bifunctor
$\mathrm{dig} \otimes \mathrm{dig}$ (\texttt{tensor\_morE},
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.dig\_prom}) then $\mathrm{Seely2E}$ at the
unit-ball points $x_1!, x_2!$, to $\langle x_1!, x_2! \rangle!$. The
left-hand side reduces, through $\mathrm{Seely2E}$ at $x_1, x_2$, then
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.dig\_prom}, then
\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.bang\_fmap\_prom} on the projection tuple
(\autoref{def:bang-proj-tuple}), to the same $\langle x_1!, x_2!
\rangle!$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{The remaining Melli\`es coherence squares.}
The remaining new-Seely-category coherence laws are all proved, each by
the \emph{same} recipe: agree on $x_1! \otimes x_2!$ (or its ternary
analogue \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.tens\_excl\_charact3}, the $n = 3$ case
of \autoref{lem:tens-excl-charact}), then reduce both sides by
$\mathrm{Seely2E}$, $\mathrm{dig\_prom}$, $\mathrm{bang\_fmap\_prom}$ and
$\texttt{tensor\_morE}$ --- exactly as the paper's ``easy to prove'' by
Lemma \texttt{tens-excl-equal-charact}. Concretely: the strong-monoidal
coherence of $(\mathord{!}, \mathrm{Seely2}, \mathrm{Seely0})$ against the
tensor's $\alpha/\sigma/\lambda/\rho$ ---
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_assoc},
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_braid},
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_lunit},
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_runit}; and the counit
compatibility \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_der\_unit} (for the unit
iso) together with \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_der1} /
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_der2} (the binary counit square). The one
square not phrased as a single morphism equation is the un-projected
binary counit $\mathrm{der}_{B_1 \mathbin{\&} B_2} \circ \mathrm{Seely2}
: \mathord{!}B_1 \otimes \mathord{!}B_2 \to B_1 \mathbin{\&} B_2$: since
$\mathbin{\&}$ and $\otimes$ differ and there is no canonical comparison
$B_1 \mathbin{\&} B_2 \leftrightarrow B_1 \otimes B_2$ to phrase it
through, it is recorded in projected form ($\mathrm{seely\_der1}$ /
$\mathrm{seely\_der2}$, one equation per projection), which carries the
same content --- a product map is determined by its projections. Nothing
is axiomatised beyond the staged Seely isos.

\subsection{$\ICones$ is a Seely category}\label{sec:seely-thm}

\begin{theorem}[$\ICones$ is a Seely category, paper \S9
(ref.\ Melli\`es~\cite{mellies-categorical-semantics})]\label{thm:seely}
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.ICones\_Seely}\rocqok
\uses{thm:thm515, def:comonad-record, thm:scones-prod, def:seely2,
lem:seely-comult}
$\ICones$ is a Seely category: the exponential comonad $\mathord{!}$ is
a strong monoidal comonad from the cartesian $(\mathbin{\&}, \top)$ to
the tensor $(\otimes, 1)$. The structure is bundled in the record
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.SeelyCategory}, whose canonical witness
\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.ICones\_Seely} populates every field with the
proved data:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the symmetric monoidal closed structure of
    Chapter~\ref{ch:tensor} (\rocq{Icones.homs.smcc.ICones\_SMCC},
    \autoref{thm:thm515}) --- $\otimes$, the unit $1$, the bifunctor
    action and the $\alpha/\lambda/\rho/\sigma$ coherence;
  \item the exponential comonad $\mathord{!}$
    (\rocq{Icones.homs.bang.Comonad}, \autoref{def:comonad-record}) ---
    $\mathord{!}$, $\mathord{!}f$, $\mathrm{der}$, $\mathrm{dig}$ and the
    comonad laws;
  \item the binary product $\mathbin{\&}$ (\texttt{sprod} of
    \autoref{thm:scones-prod}) --- the cartesian structure the Seely
    iso relates to the tensor;
  \item the binary Seely iso $\mathrm{Seely2}$ (\autoref{def:seely2})
    with its characterisation on promoted pure tensors and its
    naturality (the staged Yoneda data);
  \item the unit Seely iso $\mathrm{Seely0} : 1 \cong \mathord{!}\top$
    (\rocq{Icones.axioms.seely\_interface.Seely0}) over the terminal cone
    \rocq{Icones.axioms.seely\_interface.Stop}, with its characterisation
    $\mathrm{Seely0}(t) = t \cdot 0!$;
  \item the full strong-monoidal coherence ---
    \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_assoc},
    \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_braid},
    \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_lunit},
    \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_runit} --- and the counit
    compatibility (\rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_der\_unit},
    \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_der1},
    \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.seely\_der2}) alongside the comultiplication
    coherence (\autoref{lem:seely-comult}), every law derived via
    \autoref{lem:tens-excl-charact} (and its ternary form).

\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Each field is one of the proved lemmas above (or a staged Seely datum);
the witness \rocq{Icones.homs.seely.ICones\_Seely} just assembles them.
The tensor and the exponential are now discharged
(Chapters~\ref{ch:tensor},~\ref{ch:exponential}, \S\ref{sec:exp-staging}),
so the \emph{only} unproved inputs are the staged Seely-iso existence
symbols (\texttt{Seely2}/\texttt{Seely0}), to be discharged with the
remaining M-SAFT/Yoneda content.
\end{proof}
