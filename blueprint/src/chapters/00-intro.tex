\chapter*{Introduction}

This blueprint accompanies a Rocq formalisation of \emph{Integration in
Cones} by Thomas Ehrhard and Guillaume Geoffroy
(\cite{ehrhard-geoffroy-icones}). It describes the mathematics of the
formalisation in mathematical English, and links each definition,
lemma, proposition and theorem to the corresponding Rocq declaration
in the source tree under \texttt{theories/}.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The Rocq development covers paper \S2--\S9 (see \texttt{PLAN.md} at the
root of the repository), plus a beyond-the-paper call-by-value model
(Chapter~\ref{ch:cbv}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item paper \S2 --- cones, the category $\Cones$ (M1);
  \item paper \S3 --- measurable cones, paths, $\FMeas$, the
    category $\MCones$ (M2);
  \item paper \S4 --- the Pettis-style integral, integrable cones,
    cone-Fubini, completeness and well-poweredness of $\ICones$ (M3);
  \item paper \S5.1 + \S5.2 --- the concrete internal hom
    $A \lin B$ as a $\Cone$, $\MCone$, then $\ICone$ structure (M4);
  \item paper \S6 --- the substochastic-kernel category $\Skern$
    and the fully-faithful embedding $\Skern \hookrightarrow \ICones$
    (Theorem~6.5, the axiom-free regression anchor, M5);
  \item paper \S5.3--\S5.5 --- the tensor product $\otimes$ and the
    symmetric monoidal closed structure (Theorem~5.15,
    Chapter~\ref{ch:tensor}), now \emph{axiom-free};
  \item paper \S7 --- the stable cartesian closed category
    $\mathbf{SCones}$ (Theorem~7.32, Chapter~\ref{ch:stable});
  \item paper \S9 --- the exponential $\mathord{!}$ comonad, the Seely
    category (Theorem~9.5) and the $\mathord{!}$-coalgebra structure of
    $\FMeas$ (Theorem~9.7, Chapter~\ref{ch:exponential}), plus the \S9.2
    fixpoints;
  \item \emph{beyond the paper} --- a \emph{call-by-value} model
    following Melli\`es~\cite{mellies-categorical-semantics} \S7.4 (the
    Eilenberg--Moore / coalgebra route), the paper's stated future work
    (Chapter~\ref{ch:cbv}): the \emph{full} cartesian
    Eilenberg--Moore category of $\mathord{!}$ carried by the linear
    $\otimes$ (Melli\`es' Cor 17/20, via the structural retraction-and-
    lifting argument), the cartesian $\eta$-rule
    $\langle \pi_1, \pi_2 \rangle = \mathrm{id}$ on EM(!) (Fox 1976 /
    Melli\`es Prop 28, to our knowledge a first Coq / Rocq
    formalisation), the LNL monoidal adjunction
    $U \dashv \widetilde{\mathord{!}}$ to $\ICones$, a CBV
    value-fixpoint at function types (\texttt{Yfix\_fun\_T},
    folklore per P.-A.~Melli\`es 2026-05-31, also a first Coq / Rocq
    formalisation), and a higher-order \emph{direct-style}
    (Plotkin/Girard CBV), \emph{multi-variable named-variable}
    QBS-mirror PPL with \emph{implicit effects}
    (\texttt{theories/programs/ppl.v}, in the Saito--Affeldt APLAS
    2023 surface style with canonical-structure-driven variable
    lookup and a custom-entry surface notation; no $\mathtt{tprob}$
    type marker, no syntactic $\mathtt{return}$, no $\mathtt{bind}$ --
    the probability monad lives in the interpretation $\mathtt{eD}$,
    not the source types) supporting OCaml-style $\mathtt{let\,rec}$
    (\texttt{ne\_fix}) and the boolean cascade
    (\texttt{tbool} / \texttt{ne\_true} / \texttt{ne\_false} /
    \texttt{ne\_bernoulli} / \texttt{ne\_if}, the last dispatched via
    the EM-Kleisli \texttt{case\_em} on the 2-point cone of paper \S4.4
    / Theorem 4.24). Six end-to-end examples in
    \texttt{theories/programs/examples.v}: the three QBS-style headlines
    (\texttt{ex\_random\_constant}, \texttt{ex\_random\_linear},
    \texttt{ex\_bayes\_linear} --- the unnormalised
    prior/score/observe posterior, the only example exercising the
    term-level Bayesian-score constructor) plus the three Phase 4
    productive partial-termination examples
    (\texttt{ex\_loop} = bare divergence,
    \texttt{ex\_geom} = geometric counter,
    \texttt{ex\_almost\_loop} = parameterised partial termination via
    $\mathtt{ne\_fix} + \mathtt{ne\_if} + \mathtt{ne\_bernoulli}$),
    all axiom-free.
\end{itemize}
The whole development is \textbf{fully axiom-free}: every result rests
on \emph{zero} project axioms, using only the three classical-logic
axioms inherited from \mathcompA{}
(\texttt{propositional\_extensionality},
\texttt{functional\_extensionality\_dep},
\texttt{constructive\_indefinite\_description}). An earlier iteration
framed the project as an \emph{axiom-free core} versus a \emph{staged
tier} of SAFT/Yoneda representability content (the tensor $\otimes$, the
exponential $\mathord{!}$, the Seely isomorphisms) parked behind
\texttt{Parameter} interfaces under \texttt{theories/axioms/}; that tier
has now been \emph{entirely discharged} by the concrete M-SAFT
construction. There is no staged tier and no \texttt{theories/axioms/}
directory: the linear-logic model \emph{through} paper~\S9 is complete
and axiom-free. Beyond the paper, the call-by-value \emph{model
structure} of Chapter~\ref{ch:cbv} is also built and axiom-free: the
\emph{full} Eilenberg--Moore category $\mathrm{EM}(\mathord{!})$ is
cartesian with product the linear $\otimes$ (Melli\`es' Proposition
26--28 / Corollary 17/20 by the structural retraction-and-lifting
argument), the cartesian $\eta$-rule
$\langle \pi_1, \pi_2 \rangle = \mathrm{id}$ holds on \emph{every}
coalgebra pair (Fox 1976 / Melli\`es Prop 28 at the icones level, a
first Coq / Rocq formalisation),
$U \dashv \widetilde{\mathord{!}}$ is a genuine LNL
adjunction with \emph{the} category of $\mathord{!}$-coalgebras, a small
first-order CBV calculus is interpreted soundly, a CBV
value-fixpoint at function types (\texttt{Yfix\_fun\_T}, folklore per
P.-A.~Melli\`es 2026-05-31, also a first Coq / Rocq formalisation)
supports OCaml-style $\mathtt{let\,rec}$, and a higher-order
\emph{direct-style, multi-variable, named-variable QBS-mirror PPL with
implicit effects} (\texttt{theories/programs/ppl.v}) using the EM(!)
\emph{Kleisli exponentials}
$\widetilde{\mathord{!}}(U A \multimap U(T\,B))$ for the
CBV monad $T = \widetilde{\mathord{!}} \circ U$ interprets six
end-to-end examples (collected in
\texttt{theories/programs/examples.v}): the three QBS-style headlines
\texttt{ex\_random\_constant} (the flagship distribution over a
function space), \texttt{ex\_random\_linear} (the killer demo: a
distribution over $\lambda x.\,m\cdot x + b$ for $m, b \sim \mu$),
and \texttt{ex\_bayes\_linear} (the textbook prior/score/observe
Bayesian shape, the only example exercising the term-level
Bayesian-score constructor; the \emph{unnormalised} posterior of
total mass $\int f(m)\,\mathrm{d}\mu(m)$),
plus the three Phase 4 productive partial-termination programs ---
\texttt{ex\_loop} (bare divergence
$\mathtt{(let\,rec\,l = \lambda\_.\,l\,())\,()}$),
\texttt{ex\_geom} (a fair-coin geometric counter
$\mathtt{(let\,rec\,g = \lambda\_.\,if\,Bernoulli(\tfrac12)\,then\,0\,
else\,1 + g\,())\,()}$, total mass 1, almost-surely terminating), and
\texttt{ex\_almost\_loop}\,$p$ (parameterised partial termination with
continuation probability $1 - p$, total mass $p$). The named surface is
the \emph{only} syntax of \texttt{theories/programs/ppl.v}: variable
lookup \texttt{\#~"x"} is canonical-structure-driven and a custom-entry
notation supports
\texttt{let "x" := M in N} (the direct-style CBV sequencer, semantically
$\mathtt{kbind\_ext}$)
/ \texttt{\textbackslash "x" ::: A => M}
/ \texttt{fix "f" ::: tfun A B in M} (OCaml-style $\mathtt{let\,rec}$)
/ \texttt{Sample} / \texttt{Score \{ f, \ldots\} e}
/ \texttt{True} / \texttt{False} / \texttt{Bernoulli \{ p, \ldots \}}
/ \texttt{if e then M else N}
/ arithmetic, in the Saito--Affeldt APLAS 2023 style. There is
\emph{no} \texttt{Ret} surface form --- the direct-style move is what
the cones model makes possible: where older LL/comonad models forced a
monadic-style source with $T$-marked function types and explicit
\texttt{return}/\texttt{bind}, the value-CCC structure on EM(!) (via
Cor 20) allows the source type $\mathtt{tfun}\,A\,B$ to interpret
directly as the Kleisli exponential
$\widetilde{\mathord{!}}(U A \multimap U(T B))$, with the monad moved
out of the source type and into the interpretation $\mathtt{eD}$.
The arithmetic primitives \texttt{ne\_add} / \texttt{ne\_mul} are
interpreted via the FMeas lax symmetric monoidal map
(\texttt{theories/homs/fmeas\_lax.v}, built on the discharged
\texttt{int\_to\_linhom\_pres\_path\_in\_cone} follow-up to paper
Theorem 6.1) and the Moggi-Kleisli Dirac/integration view of
\texttt{bind}; the term-level Bayesian-score constructor reuses
the same paper-\S6 follow-up to package its density as a path into
the unit cone. $\mathrm{EM}(\mathord{!})$ is not (and is not expected
to be) cartesian closed; what direct-style CBV actually needs are
Kleisli exponentials, and those it does have. What remains open is
genuinely beyond \S9: paper
\S8 (analytic functions, the category $\mathrm{ACONES}$), the \S9
Eilenberg–Moore full-subcategory theorem ($\mathrm{ARCAT}$ on Polish /
standard-Borel spaces $\hookrightarrow \mathrm{EM}(\mathord{!})$,
needing a Polish / standard-Borel layer not yet formalised) and the
PCS embedding (\S10).

\paragraph{How to read this blueprint.}
Each chapter mirrors a paper section (or, in some cases, a small
group of related Rocq source files). Inside each chapter, mathematical
statements are presented as \texttt{lemma} / \texttt{theorem} /
\texttt{definition} environments, each tagged with the corresponding
Rocq declaration via \verb|\rocq{...}|. The \verb|\rocqok| marker
means the surrounding environment is fully formalised and closed by
\verb|Qed| in the source tree. \verb|\uses{...}| declarations
populate the dependency graph shown in the web version of the
blueprint.

\paragraph{Status.}
All chapters are populated with the actual statements and informal
proofs, mirroring the Rocq sources. The development is
\texttt{Admitted}-free and \textbf{fully axiom-free}: it uses only the
three classical-logic axioms inherited from \texttt{mathcomp-analysis},
with \emph{zero} project axioms; see each chapter's closing ``Status''
section for the precise per-chapter axiom budget.

\paragraph{Alternative entry point: the auditor dashboard.}
A complementary, interactive way to browse the formalisation is the
\textbf{auditor dashboard}, with two tabs:
\begin{itemize}
    \item the \textbf{Paper tab}
    (\url{https://llm4rocq.github.io/icones-rocq/auditor/paper/}) gives
    a paper-to-Rocq correspondence for \S\S2--9 plus the paper-cited
    meta-theorems we had to mechanise (SAFT, Melli\`es \S7.4 Prop 28 /
    Cor 20, the LNL adjunction);
    \item the \textbf{PPL tab}
    (\url{https://llm4rocq.github.io/icones-rocq/auditor/ppl/}) is a
    top-down narrative of the probabilistic programming language
    developed on top of the paper's model --- both the call-by-value
    interpretation via $\mathrm{EM}(\mathord{!})$ (the principal
    interpretation for non-recursive QBS-style examples) and the
    call-by-name interpretation via $\mathrm{SCones}$ (which obtains
    free recursion at every function type via the paper \S9.2 fixpoint
    operator). Each entry on either tab links to the Rocq source on
    GitHub and to the rendered coqdoc page, and exposes
    \texttt{Print Assumptions} for spot-checking.
\end{itemize}
The blueprint and the dashboard are independent renderings of the
formalisation: the blueprint is paper-shaped (paper sections, English
proofs, dependency graph), the dashboard is reference-shaped
(per-entry cards with status badges, searchable, deep-linkable).
