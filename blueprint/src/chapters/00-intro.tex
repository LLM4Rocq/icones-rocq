\chapter*{Introduction}

This blueprint accompanies a Rocq formalisation of \emph{Integration in
Cones} by Thomas Ehrhard and Guillaume Geoffroy
(\cite{ehrhard-geoffroy-icones}). It describes the mathematics of the
formalisation in mathematical English, and links each definition,
lemma, proposition and theorem to the corresponding Rocq declaration
in the source tree under \texttt{theories/}.

\paragraph{Scope.}
The Rocq development covers paper \S2--\S9 (see \texttt{PLAN.md} at the
root of the repository), plus a beyond-the-paper call-by-value model
(Chapter~\ref{ch:cbv}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item paper \S2 --- cones, the category $\Cones$ (M1);
  \item paper \S3 --- measurable cones, paths, $\FMeas$, the
    category $\MCones$ (M2);
  \item paper \S4 --- the Pettis-style integral, integrable cones,
    cone-Fubini, completeness and well-poweredness of $\ICones$ (M3);
  \item paper \S5.1 + \S5.2 --- the concrete internal hom
    $A \lin B$ as a $\Cone$, $\MCone$, then $\ICone$ structure (M4);
  \item paper \S6 --- the substochastic-kernel category $\Skern$
    and the fully-faithful embedding $\Skern \hookrightarrow \ICones$
    (Theorem~6.5, the axiom-free regression anchor, M5);
  \item paper \S5.3--\S5.5 --- the tensor product $\otimes$ and the
    symmetric monoidal closed structure (Theorem~5.15,
    Chapter~\ref{ch:tensor}), now \emph{axiom-free};
  \item paper \S7 --- the stable cartesian closed category
    $\mathbf{SCones}$ (Theorem~7.32, Chapter~\ref{ch:stable});
  \item paper \S9 --- the exponential $\mathord{!}$ comonad, the Seely
    category (Theorem~9.5) and the $\mathord{!}$-coalgebra structure of
    $\FMeas$ (Theorem~9.7, Chapter~\ref{ch:exponential}), plus the \S9.2
    fixpoints;
  \item \emph{beyond the paper} --- a \emph{call-by-value} model
    following Melli\`es~\cite{mellies-categorical-semantics} \S7.4 (the
    Eilenberg--Moore / coalgebra route), the paper's stated future work
    (Chapter~\ref{ch:cbv}): the \emph{full} cartesian
    Eilenberg--Moore category of $\mathord{!}$ carried by the linear
    $\otimes$ (Melli\`es' Cor 17/20, via the structural retraction-and-
    lifting argument), the LNL monoidal adjunction
    $U \dashv \widetilde{\mathord{!}}$ to $\ICones$, a higher-order
    QBS-mirror PPL (\texttt{theories/programs/ppl.v}) interpreting
    \texttt{ex\_random\_constant}, \texttt{ex\_random\_linear} and the
    Bayesian \texttt{ex\_bayes\_linear} (the unnormalised
    prior/score/return posterior, exercising the term-level score
    constructor \texttt{e\_score\_tm}) axiom-free, plus a
    Saito--Affeldt-style named-variable surface companion
    (\texttt{theories/programs/ppl\_named.v}).
\end{itemize}
The whole development is \textbf{fully axiom-free}: every result rests
on \emph{zero} project axioms, using only the three classical-logic
axioms inherited from \mathcompA{}
(\texttt{propositional\_extensionality},
\texttt{functional\_extensionality\_dep},
\texttt{constructive\_indefinite\_description}). An earlier iteration
framed the project as an \emph{axiom-free core} versus a \emph{staged
tier} of SAFT/Yoneda representability content (the tensor $\otimes$, the
exponential $\mathord{!}$, the Seely isomorphisms) parked behind
\texttt{Parameter} interfaces under \texttt{theories/axioms/}; that tier
has now been \emph{entirely discharged} by the concrete M-SAFT
construction. There is no staged tier and no \texttt{theories/axioms/}
directory: the linear-logic model \emph{through} paper~\S9 is complete
and axiom-free. Beyond the paper, the call-by-value \emph{model
structure} of Chapter~\ref{ch:cbv} is also built and axiom-free: the
\emph{full} Eilenberg--Moore category $\mathrm{EM}(\mathord{!})$ is
cartesian with product the linear $\otimes$ (Melli\`es' Proposition
26--28 / Corollary 17/20 by the structural retraction-and-lifting
argument), $U \dashv \widetilde{\mathord{!}}$ is a genuine LNL
adjunction with \emph{the} category of $\mathord{!}$-coalgebras, a small
first-order CBV calculus is interpreted soundly, and a higher-order
\emph{direct-style multi-variable QBS-mirror PPL}
(\texttt{theories/programs/ppl.v}) using the EM(!) \emph{Kleisli
exponentials} $\widetilde{\mathord{!}}(U A \multimap U B)$ for the
CBV monad $T = \widetilde{\mathord{!}} \circ U$ interprets three
QBS-style headline examples:
\texttt{ex\_random\_constant} (the flagship distribution over a
function space), \texttt{ex\_random\_linear} (the killer demo: a
distribution over $\lambda x.\,m\cdot x + b$ for $m, b \sim \mu$),
and \texttt{ex\_bayes\_linear} (the textbook prior/score/return
Bayesian shape, the first example exercising the term-level score
\texttt{e\_score\_tm}; the \emph{unnormalised} posterior of total
mass $\int f(m)\,\mathrm{d}\mu(m)$).
A Saito--Affeldt-style named-variable surface companion
(\texttt{theories/programs/ppl\_named.v}) delegates its semantics
structurally to the same \texttt{eD}.
The arithmetic primitives \texttt{e\_add} / \texttt{e\_mul} are
interpreted via the FMeas lax symmetric monoidal map
(\texttt{theories/homs/fmeas\_lax.v}, built on the discharged
\texttt{int\_to\_linhom\_pres\_path\_in\_cone} follow-up to paper
Theorem 6.1) and the Moggi-Kleisli Dirac/integration view of
\texttt{bind}; the term-level score \texttt{e\_score\_tm} reuses the
same paper-\S6 follow-up to package its density as a path into the
unit cone. $\mathrm{EM}(\mathord{!})$ is not (and is not expected
to be) cartesian closed; what Moggi-CBV actually needs are Kleisli
exponentials, and those it does have. What remains open is genuinely
beyond \S9: paper
\S8 (analytic functions, the category $\mathrm{ACONES}$), the \S9
Eilenberg–Moore full-subcategory theorem ($\mathrm{ARCAT}$ on Polish /
standard-Borel spaces $\hookrightarrow \mathrm{EM}(\mathord{!})$,
needing a Polish / standard-Borel layer not yet formalised) and the
PCS embedding (\S10).

\paragraph{How to read this blueprint.}
Each chapter mirrors a paper section (or, in some cases, a small
group of related Rocq source files). Inside each chapter, mathematical
statements are presented as \texttt{lemma} / \texttt{theorem} /
\texttt{definition} environments, each tagged with the corresponding
Rocq declaration via \verb|\rocq{...}|. The \verb|\rocqok| marker
means the surrounding environment is fully formalised and closed by
\verb|Qed| in the source tree. \verb|\uses{...}| declarations
populate the dependency graph shown in the web version of the
blueprint.

\paragraph{Status.}
All chapters are populated with the actual statements and informal
proofs, mirroring the Rocq sources. The development is
\texttt{Admitted}-free and \textbf{fully axiom-free}: it uses only the
three classical-logic axioms inherited from \texttt{mathcomp-analysis},
with \emph{zero} project axioms; see each chapter's closing ``Status''
section for the precise per-chapter axiom budget.
